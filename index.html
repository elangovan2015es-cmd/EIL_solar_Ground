💡 Why does reducing debt extend the payback period?
Payback is measured against equity invested. When you reduce debt from 70% to 50%, your equity goes from Rs 27.6 Cr to Rs 46 Cr — a 67% increase. Even though FCF improves slightly (less interest expense), you now have much more equity to recover. The maths always produces a longer payback when equity is higher. Lower debt reduces lender risk and improves loan terms, but it always extends equity payback. This is correct financial behaviour, not a model error.

Regulatory
Group captive & 110 kV evacuation
Group captive CPP — Tamil Nadu
CSS — ZERO (waived)
Fully waived for Group Captive CPP under TNERC T.O. 7 of 2022. This is the primary cost advantage of the Group Captive structure over commercial open access.
Additional Surcharge — ZERO
Waived for Group Captive CPP. PSA must be signed and registered before commissioning to preserve this exemption.
OA charge all-in — Rs 1.33 per kWh
Includes wheeling, transmission, SLDC charges and deemed losses. Monetary charge — separate from physical unit deduction at injection point.
26% equity — buyer must hold
Each captive consumer must hold minimum 26% equity in the generating company under CERC Captive Rules 2001. Non-compliance immediately reinstates CSS.
51% captive consumption requirement
Minimum 51% of annual generation must be consumed by captive users. Monitor monthly and adjust buyer nominations proactively to avoid year-end shortfall.
Proportional consumption compliance
Each member must consume in proportion to equity stake. Annual compliance certificate required from the generator and submitted to TNERC.
TNEB empanelment — EIL Solar
EIL Solar / Kaveri Power Solutions is TNEB-empanelled. Maintain this empanelment continuously. Lapse in empanelment disrupts OA approval.
110 kV evacuation — this project
Evacuation at 110 kV — advantage
This project evacuates at 110 kV EHT. At 110 kV injection, TNERC deemed transmission loss is NIL. Only distribution loss of 1.52% applies to the buyer end. This is significantly better than 33 kV injection which carries 3.91% transmission loss.
TNEB DPR and REC approval — 110 kV feeder
110 kV feeder availability and substation bay allocation must be confirmed with TANTRANSCO. This is different from 33 kV and requires higher-level approval. Budget 12 to 18 months. Initiate immediately after site identification.
Step-up transformer — 11 kV to 110 kV
Plant generates at 0.69 kV (inverter output), steps up to 11 kV (plant bus), then to 110 kV for grid injection. Both transformers are part of EPC scope. Confirm transformer specifications with TNEB before EPC award.
SLDC scheduling and metering
Net injection metered at 110 kV delivery point. Day-ahead generation schedule must be filed with SLDC. Battery dispatch filed separately. SLDC meter is the settlement boundary.
Grid congestion at 110 kV level
110 kV feeders in Tamil Nadu are increasingly congested during 10 AM to 2 PM solar peak. SLDC may curtail injection. Battery storage configured to shift generation to 6 PM to 10 PM peak demand window where curtailment risk is low.
OA regulation risk
TNERC reviews OA charges every 3 years in each MYT order. OA may increase materially. The model uses 3% per year escalation. Watch TNERC filings and update accordingly.
Land use conversion
Agricultural land requires District Collector conversion order before construction. Budget 12 to 18 months. Start this process in parallel with all other approvals.
Deemed loss — update each TNERC order
Loss percentages in Table 5-12 are revised with each TNERC Tariff Order approximately every 3 years. Update the Grid Losses section whenever a new order is issued.
