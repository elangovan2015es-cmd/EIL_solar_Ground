Regulatory
Group captive, OA & compliance
Group captive CPP — Tamil Nadu
CSS — ZERO (waived)
Fully waived for Group Captive CPP under TNERC T.O. 7/2022. Major cost benefit over commercial open access.
Additional Surcharge — ZERO
Waived for Group Captive CPP. Ensure PSA is signed and registered before commissioning date.
OA charge all-in — ₹1.33 per kWh
Includes wheeling, transmission, SLDC and losses. This is a monetary charge separate from physical deemed loss deduction.
26% equity — buyer must hold
Each captive consumer must hold minimum 26% equity in the generating company under CERC Captive Rules 2001.
51% captive consumption
Minimum 51% of annual generation must be consumed by captive users. Shortfall in any year risks CSS reinstatement.
Proportional consumption compliance
Each member must consume in proportion to their equity stake. Annual compliance certificate required from the generator.
TNEB empanelment — EIL Solar
EIL Solar / Kaveri Power Solutions is TNEB-empanelled. Maintain empanelment continuously for OA benefits to continue.
Battery, evacuation & land
Grid congestion — solar hours
SLDC may curtail injection during morning peak hours. Battery discharge in evening avoids curtailment and earns peak premium.
BESS — same-day OA treatment
Power stored and discharged on same day treated as single solar OA transaction. No extra OA charge under current TNERC framework.
OA regulation risk
TNERC reviews OA charges every 3 years in each MYT order. OA may increase materially. Model uses 3% per year escalation as buffer.
HT feeder — TNEB approval
TNEB DPR and REC approval needed before substation location is confirmed. Budget 6 to 18 months. Get LOA before signing EPC contract.
Land use conversion
78 acres likely agricultural. Requires conversion order from District Collector or CMDA. Budget 12 to 18 months in project timeline.
Deemed loss — update each TNERC order
Table 5-12 loss percentages are revised in each TNERC Tariff Order approximately every 3 years. Update the Transmission Loss section whenever a new order is issued.
