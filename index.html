Group Captive CPP — TN Regulations
CSS — ZERO (waived)
Fully waived for Group Captive CPP. TNERC T.O. 7/2022. Major cost benefit vs commercial OA.
Additional Surcharge — ZERO
Waived for Group Captive CPP structure. Ensure PSA is signed before commissioning.
OA all-in ₹1.33/kWh
Includes wheeling, transmission, SLDC, losses. No separate charges. T.O. 7/2022.
26% equity — buyer must hold
Each captive consumer must hold minimum 26% equity in the generating company (CERC Captive Rules 2001).
51% captive consumption
At least 51% of annual generation must be consumed by captive users. Shortfall risks CSS reinstatement.
Proportional consumption
Each member must consume proportional to equity stake. Annual compliance certificate required.
TNEB empanelment (EIL Solar)
EIL Solar / Kaveri Power Solutions is TNEB-empanelled. Maintain empanelment for OA benefits.
Battery, Evacuation & Land
Grid congestion — solar hours
SLDC may curtail injection during morning peak. Battery evening evacuation avoids curtailment risk.
BESS — same-day OA treatment
Power stored and discharged same day treated as single solar transaction. No extra OA charge currently.
OA regulation risk
TNERC reviews OA every ~3 years (MYT). OA may rise. Model uses 3%/yr escalation as buffer.
HT feeder — TNEB approval
DPR and REC approval needed. Budget 6–18 months. Get LOA before EPC contract is signed.
Land conversion — agriculture
78 acres likely agricultural land. Requires District Collector order. Budget 12–18 months in timeline.
SLDC scheduling (BESS)
Battery dispatch needs separate day-ahead schedule filing with SLDC. Metering at delivery point.
