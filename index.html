Debt & Equity
Capital structure, O&M & taxation
Why does reducing debt extend payback? Payback is measured against equity invested. Less debt means more equity upfront. Even though FCF improves slightly (less interest), you have far more equity to recover — so payback extends. This is correct financial behaviour, not a model error. Lower debt = lower risk but longer payback.

Regulatory
Group captive & 110 kV evacuation
Group captive CPP — Tamil Nadu
CSS — ZERO (waived)
Fully waived for Group Captive CPP under TNERC T.O. 7 of 2022. Primary cost advantage over commercial OA.
Additional Surcharge — ZERO
Waived. PSA must be signed and registered before commissioning to preserve this exemption.
OA charge all-in Rs 1.33/kWh
Includes wheeling, transmission, SLDC and deemed losses. Monetary charge separate from physical unit deduction.
26% equity — each buyer must hold
Minimum 26% equity in generating company per buyer (CERC Captive Rules 2001). Non-compliance reinstates CSS immediately.
51% captive consumption
Minimum 51% of annual generation must be consumed by captive users. Monitor monthly. Shortfall in any year risks CSS reinstatement.
Proportional consumption
Each member consumes in proportion to equity stake. Annual compliance certificate required.
110 kV evacuation — this project
Transmission loss = NIL at 110 kV
110 kV injection: TNERC deemed transmission loss is ZERO. Only distribution loss (1.52% to 11 kV buyer) applies. Significantly better than 33 kV injection (3.91% transmission loss).
TANTRANSCO 110 kV bay allocation
110 kV feeder availability confirmed with TANTRANSCO before DPR submission. Budget 12 to 18 months for full approval.
Grid congestion 10 AM to 2 PM
110 kV feeders in TN increasingly congested during solar peak. SLDC may curtail. Battery shifts generation to 6 to 10 PM low-congestion window.
OA regulation risk — every 3 years
TNERC reviews OA charges each MYT order (~every 3 years). OA may increase. Model uses 3%/yr escalation as buffer. Watch TNERC filings.
