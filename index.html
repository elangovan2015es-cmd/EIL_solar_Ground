Capital structure, O&M & taxation
Why does reducing debt extend payback? Payback is measured against equity invested. Less debt means more equity upfront. Even though FCF improves slightly (less interest), you have far more equity to recover — so payback extends. This is correct financial behaviour, not a model error. Lower debt = lower risk but longer payback.

TNERC Compliance
Monthly tracking & annual TNPDCL certificate
Annual obligation: At the end of each year submit to TNPDCL that Group Captive CPP status is maintained. Three rules must be met: (1) All customers collectively hold ≥26% SPV equity. (2) Total captive consumption ≥51% of net generation. (3) Each customer consumes proportionally to their equity stake (±10% tolerance per TNERC). This section tracks all three month by month.
SPV and plant details for annual certificate
Rule 1 — Collective equity ≥26% (CERC Captive Rules 2001)
The 26% rule applies to the total of all captive customers combined. Individual customers may hold any percentage. The developer/promoter holds the remaining equity. If total customer equity falls below 26%, CSS is reinstated retroactively for the entire year.
Rule 2 & 3 — Monthly generation and consumption tracking
Enter actual monthly generation (MU) from SLDC meter. Customer-wise allotment auto-calculates from equity proportion. Enter each customer's actual consumption. Variance and compliance status shown instantly.
